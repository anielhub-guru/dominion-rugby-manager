# Roadmap

Ideas for later. Nothing here is committed or scheduled. Items marked "parked" were deliberately put on hold.

## Competitions

- **Cup competition.** A knockout cup alongside the league, giving high-stakes games each season.
- **World Cup with national teams.**
  - You become the manager of a country, for example Nigeria.
  - Players need nationalities, which the game doesn't have yet.
  - You pick a camp squad, then cut it down to the squad you take to the World Cup.
  - The tournament is knockout, and you play through it to win.

## Offers and business decisions

The theme is business management: every deal gives something and costs flexibility, so the player faces a push and pull.

- **Offers during the offseason and mid-season.** Several can arrive at once, and the player chooses which to take.
- **Sponsor and investor deals.** A shirt sponsor or an investment bank offers money in return for an obligation.
  - Example: play a named player for X matches. You can break the deal and bench the player, but then you lose the money or take a penalty. The tension is keeping the commitment against winning the league.
  - Example: take an investment but lose the ability to change clubs for 3 seasons.
- **Future-paid incentives.** The same offer, but the money arrives later if a condition is met, so one system stays interesting in many forms.
  - Pay now (built): the board or a sponsor covers the cost of a facility, sometimes in return for staying at the club.
  - Pay later: a sponsor promises money in the offseason if a named player plays X games, or if the club hits a target such as a top-three finish, X wins or a clean sheet streak.
  - Each form has a condition, a payout and a consequence if the condition is missed (no money, or a penalty).
- **Construction projects.** Built: training ground, medical centre and youth academy, with build time, a Projects tab, board proposals and genius discovery (see the changelog).

## Era starts

Start a career in a different era with the same club, so the experience differs by start year. For example, a brand-new, low-funded club in 1958, the league's best club in 1980, or a fallen giant in 2020.

- An era is a bundle of starting parameters over the same engine: funds, facilities, media visibility, and starting objectives.
- Era history is fictional and small in scale. Real events can be used as background but aren't required.
- Brief story moments between play, built around big dreams, breaking out and legacy.
- Recurring characters across eras, for example a young team captain who later becomes a board member or a sponsor.
- **Eras are time-bound, about 10 years each.** The next era arrives on schedule whether or not the player has won anything, so the game never waits on or errors over an achievement.
- **Era-specific facilities.** Each era names the same facility types differently. When a new era begins, its version of each facility replaces the old one, and the upgrades you've bought carry over.
  - Every facility has the same simple upgrade: a flat +X on top of the starter version. This works the same in any era.
  - Because facilities carry on across eras, later seasons keep their era flavour instead of settling into the same game.

## Other ideas from earlier discussion

- League history: champions by year, records, a hall of fame, retired legends.
- Player morale and traits, such as unhappiness over playing time, a captain, a fan-favourite local hero.
- Rival clubs bidding for your prospects, and contracts that run out.
- A yearly youth intake or draft event.
- A bigger league (8 clubs means the same fixtures each year).
- A live call at key match moments, such as the kick or a set play.

## Parked: story cutaways and event moments

These add no real gameplay value, and each one needs its own artwork, so a large set would be costly and could get repetitive. This is a record of what is possible.

- **Signings, by player type:** a genius player, a player from a prestigious sporting background (such as a famous family or academy), and other variations driven by personality.
- **Retirements and departures:** a retirement scene, variations for how someone leaves, a one-club career, a club legend's farewell.
- **Other club stories:** milestones and career stories in the same style.

Revisit if there is artwork to support it, or if the moments can feed into gameplay.
